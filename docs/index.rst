What Problems Can Big Fish Games Support Handle?
------------------------------------------------

Big Fish Games provides assistance for several categories of customer problems.

Account and Sign-In Problems
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

You can contact support if you cannot access your account, have forgotten your password, or are experiencing difficulties signing in.

When requesting assistance, explain what happens when you attempt to sign in. If you receive an error message, include the exact wording of that message.

Billing and Purchase Issues
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Big Fish Games support can help with questions involving purchases, billing, orders, and payment problems.

When contacting support about a purchase, include the relevant order information when available. Never provide your complete credit-card number or account password in a support request.

Game Downloads and Installation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Some players experience problems when downloading or installing games. Common symptoms include failed downloads, incomplete installations, games that do not launch, or problems with the Big Fish Games application.

Before contacting support, check the relevant troubleshooting information in the Help Center. If the problem continues, describe the steps you have already tried.

Game Crashes and Technical Errors
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Technical problems may include crashes, freezing, error messages, blank screens, or other unexpected behavior.

A useful support request should include the game title, device or computer information, operating system when relevant, exact error message, and the troubleshooting steps you have already attempted.

Game Club Questions
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Big Fish Games also provides support resources for Game Club-related questions. Customers can use the Help Center to find information about memberships, subscriptions, purchases, and related account concerns.

Information to Give Customer Support
-------------------------------------

A detailed support request can make it easier for the support team to understand and investigate your problem.

For technical problems, consider providing:

* The name of the game.
* Your computer or mobile device.
* Your operating system, when relevant.
* The exact error message.
* When the problem began.
* What happened before the problem occurred.
* Troubleshooting steps you have already tried.
* Screenshots when they help explain the problem.

For account or billing problems, you may need to provide:

* The email address associated with your account.
* Your order number, if applicable.
* A clear description of the problem.
* Relevant purchase information requested by support.

Avoid providing sensitive information such as your password, complete credit-card number, banking password, or security codes.

How to Get Faster Support
-------------------------

Although live chat may not always be available, you can improve your chances of getting a useful response by preparing your request carefully.

First, search the Help Center before submitting a ticket. Many common issues have dedicated troubleshooting articles.

Second, provide a descriptive explanation. For example, instead of writing::

    My game doesn't work.

write something more specific, such as::

    My game crashes immediately after I click Play. I reinstalled the game and restarted my computer, but the problem continues.

A specific description gives the support team more useful information from the beginning.

Third, include relevant technical or purchase information in your initial request. This can reduce the need for multiple follow-up questions.

Finally, avoid submitting multiple support tickets for the same problem. If you need to add information, use the existing support conversation or reply to the confirmation email when instructed.
